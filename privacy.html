Privacy

The Manchas Stamp Passport is made for children and their grown-ups, so we keep it very simple.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no names, no email addresses, no ads and no tracking or analytics.

Where your stamps are kept

Your stamps are saved only in this browser on this device (in its local storage). They are never sent to us or to anyone else. If you clear your browser data or switch phones, the stamps start again.

Names on certificates

A name typed on a certificate is only used to draw the picture on your device. It is not saved or sent anywhere.

Other services

The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your device's IP address to deliver them. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep basic server logs.
